This is my favorite line from a book I read lately

The best thing I read lately was The Miraculous Journey of Edward Tulane by Kate DiCamillo. One line that stayed with me was when Edward needed to learn how to love and care for someone other than himself.

I liked this part because Edward starts the story mostly about himself. As he meets different people throughout his journey, he slowly learns what love, loss, and friendship really mean.

This story made me think about how we value someone until we lose them. Edward's journey showed me that loving someone can make us vulnerable, but it can also make our lives more meaningful.
